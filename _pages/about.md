I am an M.S. student in Electrical Engineering at [KAIST](https://www.kaist.ac.kr/en/), advised by [Prof. Heejin Ahn](https://sites.google.com/view/heejinahn/home) in the [Control and Intelligent Systems Lab](https://cis.kaist.ac.kr/). Previously, I conducted autonomy research at the [Agency for Defense Development (ADD)](https://www.add.re.kr/eps). I received my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from [UNIST](https://www.unist.ac.kr/) in 2021, graduating **summa cum laude**.

My research focuses on **practical safety for learning-enabled robots**. I develop methods that account for uncertainty in neural perception and extend robot safety beyond collision avoidance to semantic risks. My work connects learned representations with reachability analysis and uncertainty-aware control.

<p class="research-directions"><strong>Current directions:</strong> Language-conditioned semantic safety and global navigation with 3D Gaussian Splatting scene representations.</p>
